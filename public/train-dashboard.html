💡 What is happening here? (click to collapse)

🧬 What's a "generation"?

The AI starts with a population of 100 random "brains" (small neural networks). Each brain plays a 60-second game. The worst 5 are discarded, the best 5 are kept, and the other 90 are bred (mix two parents, add small random mutations) to form the next generation. Repeat — the brains get smarter over time.

🏆 What's "fitness"?

A score that rewards good play:

  • +20 / +50 / +100 for destroying large / medium / small asteroids
  • ×10 per second of survival time
  • +100 per power-up picked up

Higher = smarter. The Best line is the top brain; the Average line is the whole population.

📈 How to read the chart

Cyan = best brain (champion of that generation).
Blue = average brain (whole population).
Up-and-to-the-right = learning. Flat line = stuck. Big drop = bad mutations this round.

🚀 How to use the trained brain

1. Wait for a good Best Ever score (or click Stop when you want to keep it).
2. Click Deploy to Game — saves the best brain to public/trained-genome.json.
3. Open localhost:5173 — the game will auto-load the brain.
